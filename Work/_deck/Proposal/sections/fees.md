# Fee estimate

**Two pages, last before the appendices, in every reference.** Three title it *Fee estimate (1/2)* and *(2/2)*; one calls it
*Commercials*. The anatomy is identical. This is the page the buyer turns to
second, after the summary, and the one they compare across firms cell by cell.

Opens on a `Section divider` titled *Fee estimate*, or shares a divider with the
timetable.

## Page 1 — the estimate

`Title only_Blank`. Left prose, right tables, caveat, footnote.

| Element | Geometry |
|---|---|
| Opening paragraph | y 3.70, 15.50 cm wide, left |
| Fee table | y 3.70, right. Header cell *Preliminary fee estimate* |
| Rate card | y 10.20, right, beneath the fee table. Header cell *Discounted hourly rate²* |
| Caveat | y 15.50, 12.20 cm wide |
| Footnote | y 16.30, full width |

**The opening paragraph is boilerplate.** Identical in three of four references
to the word:

> *Based on our experience on engagements of similar type and complexity and our
> current understanding of [Target / the Client], we have presented an indicative
> fee estimate in the adjacent table. We reserve the right to revise the estimate
> should the scope or our understanding change.*

**The fee table** has one row per workstream in scope order, under either two
columns — *Service | Fee (USDk)* — or four where fees are weekly ranges —
*Service | Fee basis (USDk) | Weekly – Low | Weekly – High*. Fee basis is a fixed
figure, *Time & materials¹*, or a weekly rate. Rows can group under a bold
sub-header — *Non-HR related services*, *HR related services*. A total row
appears where the work is fixed-fee.

**The rate card** lists grades in this order and no other: Partner, Director,
Associate Director, Manager, Assistant Manager, Consultant. **Rates come from
the pursuit team for each proposal.** The two references that show them differ
by roughly a fifth on every grade, so a rate carried from an earlier deck is a
wrong rate. §6 of `AGENTS.md` applies.

**The caveat** is boilerplate with one variable:

> *The final fee will be heavily dependent on the [quality of available
> information / level of support you require] and on the final scope agreed with
> you.*

**The footnote** defines the superscripts:

> *Note: ¹Time & Materials refers to work performed to be charged on a time and
> materials basis at our discounted hourly rates of the individuals who perform
> the services, as set out on this page; ²Discounted hourly rates are…*

## Page 2 — the assumptions

`Title only_Blank` or `Two Columns Text`. Two columns of 13.70 cm at y 3.70,
headed *Fee assumptions* and *Fee assumptions (cont'd)*. Prose, one assumption
per paragraph. The set the references share:

- *In estimating our costs, we take into account key factors that determine the
  timing and completion of our work, such as availability and quality of
  information, level of cooperation and complexity of the transaction.*
- *All required information and documents requested in an Information Request
  List (IRL) submitted in advance are provided prior to the commencement of our
  work.*
- *Minutes of all meetings and financial information are provided in English.*
- *In the event that any of these assumptions change, we would need to re-visit
  our fee estimate.*
- *Our ability to perform the work within the suggested timeframe will largely
  depend on the scope and the timely provision of information.*

Add engagement-specific assumptions after these. Never remove one.

## Conventions

- Currency stated once in the table header and never repeated per cell
- Fee rows match scope workstreams and timetable rows one to one, same order
- Where a *Budget* page appears in the appendix, it is the detailed build-up
  and this page is the summary of it

## Checks

- Every workstream in [[scope]] has a fee row, and the reverse
- No rate is carried forward from a previous proposal
- The summary page's fee column carries the sentence and no figure
