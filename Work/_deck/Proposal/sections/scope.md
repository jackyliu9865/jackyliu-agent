# Proposed scope of work

**The longest section, four to nineteen pages, in every reference.** It is the
part of the proposal the buyer cross-checks against the RFP line by line, and it
is where a page is missing or padded. Opens on a `Section divider` titled
*Proposed scope of work*; one reference folds fees into the divider as
*Proposed scope of work & fees*.

## Structure

One page set per workstream, each numbered as a continuation: *Financial due
diligence (1/6)*, *Tax due diligence (1/3)*, *Integration management (1/4)*.
Workstreams seen across the four:

| Deal pursuits | Strategy and integration pursuits |
|---|---|
| Financial due diligence | Integration management |
| Tax due diligence | People & Change |
| Tax structuring | Technology |
| SPA assistance | Tax |
| Technology due diligence | Phase 1, 2, 3… where the work is sequential |
| Integration due diligence | |
| Project coordination support | |

A short *Summary of key analysis areas* page can open the section where there
are more than four workstreams.

## The page

`Title only_Blank`, one table.

| Element | Geometry |
|---|---|
| Basis line, first page of each workstream only | y 3.70, full width — *"Unless otherwise noted, our [workstream] procedures will be performed on the [consolidated] financial information of Target for the financial years ended … ("FY23"), … ("FY24") and the latest available period ("YTD26") (the "Period")."* |
| Scope table | y 3.70 (or 5.60 beneath a basis line), 28.40 cm wide. Two columns: **area** in the left cell, **KPMG scope** in the right. Header cell reads *KPMG scope* |
| Rows | Two to four per page. Left cell a short area name — *Summary financials*, *Business overview*, *Partners & payment providers*. Right cell a paragraph of procedures |

The table is the page. There is no strapline and no read column. Source line
where a figure appears.

## Writing the right-hand cell

Procedures, in the future tense, addressed to what KPMG will do: *"Obtain and
review…"*, *"Discuss with Target management to understand…"*, *"Analyse…"*,
*"Provide weekly progress updates…"*. Three to six sentences per cell. Every cell
answers *what will you actually do*, and a cell that describes an outcome rather
than an action is rewritten.

Period definitions (*FY23*, *YTD26*, *the Period*) are set once in the basis
line and used unchanged thereafter.

## Conventions

- Workstream order matches the team page and the fee table
- Where the RFP set out areas of scope, follow its order and its headings, and
  say so on the summary page — one reference does exactly this *"for ease of
  your cross-check"*
- Continuation numbering counts pages within a workstream, never across the
  section

## Checks

- Every workstream in the fee table has scope pages, and the reverse
- Every area the RFP names appears in a left-hand cell
- No right-hand cell repeats another. Where two workstreams share a procedure,
  one says *"as above"*
