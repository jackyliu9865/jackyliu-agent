## What has to be decided before this file is written

- **Section skeleton.** The order a KPMG HK proposal runs in: understanding of
  the issue, approach and methodology, workplan and timeline, team and
  credentials, fees, assumptions, terms. Confirm the order and what is mandatory.
- **Layout repertoire.** Which layouts carry approach, team and credentials.
  Likely the `Key findings_*` block family and `Two Columns Text`, with
  `Summary Financials_5 blocks` for fees.
- **Fixed content.** A proposal repeats firm boilerplate: credentials, quality
  statements, independence wording. Where does that live so it is reused rather
  than retyped.
- **Density.** A proposal is skimmed first and read closely in two or three
  sections. Which sections take the density.
- **Fee page.** The layout, and whether the numbers come from a model.
