# Line, labelled at its own end

> Slide 8 of 11, on layout `Title only_Blank`.

**A legend the eye has to travel to is a tax on every reader.**

## Exhibit 1

**The two curves separated by two orders of magnitude**

*2015-2025, indexed cost per unit of effect, log scale*

- Chart ID: `SELFTEST_LINE`
- Series: Attacker cost per effect, Defender cost per intercept
- Categories (6): 2015, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023, 2025
- Position: 2.73, 4.87 cm, 13.70 x 10.75 cm

## The read

- Each line carries its own name and final value
- A log axis is disclosed in the basis line, because the reader otherwise misreads the gaps
- Bars are never put on a log axis: length encodes value and a log axis has no zero
